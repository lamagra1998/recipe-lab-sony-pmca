## 🎯 What Is This?

Recipe Lab is a free Android app that puts **77 professional film simulation recipes** directly onto your Sony A6000 camera. You know how photographers talk about "film looks" – that warm vintage feel, the crisp contrast of classic Kodak, the moody tones of Fujifilm? This app gives you all of that without needing a computer or expensive editing software.

Instead of carrying around a notebook with camera settings, Recipe Lab stores everything *inside your camera*. You flip through looks like you flip through filters on your phone. It's that simple.

**Q: Do I need a computer?**
No. The app handles everything through your phone.
